The asymmetry

Seed returns 15.9× even in the conservative case. Series B returns 1.1× in that same case — barely capital back after three years.

Early money is protected by entry price. Late money is a bet that growth continues exactly as modelled.

A 55% failure weighting is deliberately harsh. The fund maths works through portfolio construction, not because this company is likely to succeed.

Most decks show only the success case. Showing the weighted case — and naming the 55% out loud — is what a partner will remember from the meeting.
