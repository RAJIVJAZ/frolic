Making a drink taste good once, in a kitchen, is not difficult. Making the same drink ten thousand times, at a stable cost, with a twelve-month shelf life and a supply chain that does not fail in May, is a different problem entirely.

That second problem is the one I run every day — and I run the whole of it, not a function within it.

Formulation, cold chain, procurement, batch consistency, margins that move on the price of packaging. Two years is not a long record, but it is a complete one — there is no part of a food manufacturing business I have not personally run.

The gap, stated

A solo founder is a risk and it would be strange to claim otherwise. Two capabilities are missing in-house and are being hired before the seed closes: beverage formulation and FMCG distribution. Neither can be substituted with capital.

Anuradha Enterprises operates today. FROLIC is a separate venture being built alongside it.

Expect "only two years?" — the answer is on the slide: short but complete, with no function delegated.
