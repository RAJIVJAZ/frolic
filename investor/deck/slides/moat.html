Moat

Brand, taste and flavour IP. Everything else is absent or borrowed.

Durable

Brand · 4/5

Complete original system, ten flavour worlds. The only thing in beverage that compounds and cannot be bought quickly.

Taste · unproven

Potentially the deepest moat — formulation takes years to copy. Currently a hypothesis, not an asset.

Flavour IP · 3/5

Seasonal jamun and kokum sourcing is genuinely hard. Locking it early constrains fast followers.

Builds over time

Distribution · 1/5

Zero today. Becomes a moat at Year 4+ with shelf contracts. This is what Series B buys.

Community · 3/5

Subscription, rewards and referral create switching cost. Real but modest — beverage loyalty is weak.

Supply chain · 1/5

Co-packed. No advantage today.

Not a moat

Health spec · 2/5

7 g fibre / 4 g sugar is matched by Misfits and beaten on sugar. Any competent formulator replicates it in six months.

Cost · 1/5

Subscale. We will never beat a multinational on cost and should not try.

Stating this honestly is the point. A deck that claims six moats has none.

Strategic implication: convert brand and taste into distribution and habit before an incumbent decides the category is worth entering.

Three columns, honestly graded. The "not a moat" column is what makes the "durable" column believable.
