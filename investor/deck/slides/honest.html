Before you ask

We are not India's first prebiotic soda. That is a better story.

Who is already here

Misfits — Mumbai, seed-funded by Nu Ventures and angels. 250 ml, 7 g fibre, 4 g sugar. Sells on Amazon and Instamart. Its spec is ours, at a lower price, with zero added sugar.

Bubz — a second entrant, positioned at Gen Z.

Neither has national distribution. Neither has scale.

Why that helps us

Someone else is paying for category education. Demand is validated without us funding the experiment.

First-movers in consumer categories are routinely overtaken. The winner takes taste and distribution, not the first press release.

Our wedge is not being first. It is being the only brand in the category that tastes Indian.

Lead with this, do not bury it. A partner who finds Misfits themselves after you claimed primacy will end the meeting. Volunteering it buys credibility for everything that follows — and the zero-sugar disadvantage is disclosed here too, on purpose.
