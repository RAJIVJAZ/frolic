The structural fact most decks miss

₹37 of every ₹130 can is tax, before anyone is paid.

From 22 September 2025 India taxes aerated sweetened beverages at a flat 40% GST, replacing 28% plus 12% cess. Non-carbonated fruit-based drinks sit at 5%.
