Net realisation falls as the mix shifts to retail. COGS falls faster. The expansion is a procurement achievement, not a pricing one.

Year-1 costs are co-packer tolling estimates. One production run converts the whole stack from estimate to fact.

The fibre-is-12%-of-COGS point usually surprises people and is worth pausing on. It kills the "just use less fibre" suggestion before it is made.
