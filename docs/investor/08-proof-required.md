# 08 · Proof Required Before Funding

The single most useful document in this pack. Everything else is a model;
this is the work.

## Sequencing principle

Do not raise ₹10 Cr now. The Investor Readiness Score today is **5.7/10**,
dragged down by zero traction and an undocumented team. The sequence that
works:

```
  NOW          ₹1.5–2.5 Cr angel / pre-seed
               └─ 1 production run · 3 SKUs · 2 cities · 6 months D2C
                     ↓
  MONTH 9      Cohort + taste data in hand
                     ↓
  MONTH 10     ₹10 Cr institutional seed at ₹50 Cr post
               └─ Readiness score at this point: 7.5–8 / 10
```

Raising the seed on a model alone, against a funded incumbent with a
near-identical product, invites a valuation that permanently damages the cap
table — or more likely, a pass.

## 1. Product validation

| Evidence | Specification | Why it decides the deal |
|---|---|---|
| **Blind taste test** | n ≥ 200, 5 metros, FROLIC vs Misfits vs a mainstream cola. Monadic, blind, third-party administered. Target: **≥ 60% preference vs Misfits** on at least 3 SKUs | This is the whole thesis. Taste is the only durable moat FROLIC claims. If it loses blind, nothing else in this pack matters |
| **Sugar objection test** | Does 4 g added sugar cost preference against a 0 g competitor when disclosed? | Misfits' cleaner claim is a live competitive threat; quantify it before an investor does |
| **Repeat purchase** | ≥ 35% of first-time buyers order again within 90 days | The model's central assumption. Currently unvalidated |
| **Shelf-life validation** | 12 months ambient, accelerated + real-time. Fibre suspension stability, carbonation retention, flavour degradation | A separating batch is a recall. Non-negotiable before retail |
| **FSSAI compliance** | Licence, label approval, nutrition panel verification, claims review against FSS (Advertising & Claims) Regulations 2018 | Table stakes. Absence is disqualifying |
| **Formulation defensibility** | Documented process IP; ideally a provisional patent on the fibre suspension method | Converts "taste" from a hypothesis into an asset |

> **Run the blind test first.** It is the cheapest experiment with the
> highest information value. If FROLIC does not beat Misfits on taste, the
> correct response is to reformulate, not to fundraise.

## 2. Market validation

| Evidence | Specification | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **D2C revenue** | ≥ ₹40 L cumulative over 6 months, ≥ ₹12 L in month 6 | Demonstrates demand at full price without trade support |
| **Cohort retention** | M1 / M3 / M6 curves by acquisition channel; M3 ≥ 35% | Distinguishes a brand from a promotion |
| **CAC by channel** | Blended ≤ ₹900 at ₹12 L/month scale | The model assumes ₹850. Prove it holds beyond the first ₹2 L of spend |
| **Basket size** | ≥ 10 cans/order sustained | Below 8, D2C gross margin breaks — see [05](05-unit-economics.md) |
| **Subscription attach** | ≥ 20% of orders by month 6 | The LTV case depends on it |
| **Retail sell-through** | 50-store pilot, ≥ 4 units/store/week sustained 8 weeks | The number every distributor and every consumer investor asks for |
| **Quick commerce velocity** | Listed on ≥ 2 platforms in 2 cities, repeat-order rate tracked | The realistic scale channel |
| **Distributor interest** | ≥ 3 signed LOIs from regional distributors | Evidence that trade will carry it |

**Deliberately excluded:** vanity metrics. Instagram followers, waitlist
signups, press mentions and "interest" from retailers do not appear on this
list because no competent investor counts them.

## 4. Founder validation

**This is the largest gap in the pack, and it cannot be closed by analysis.**
Nothing in this material documents who is building FROLIC. For a consumer
brand, investors back the operator before the idea.

| Requirement | Why it matters | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Beverage formulation capability** | 7 g of fibre in a stable carbonated liquid is a hard technical problem — 11 formulation rounds per the brand story | In-house food technologist, or a named consulting formulator with a track record |
| **FMCG distribution relationships** | Nobody builds Indian beverage distribution from a cold start | A co-founder or first hire from a beverage/FMCG sales organisation |
| **Manufacturing capability** | Co-packer selection, QA, batch control, recall procedure | Signed co-packer agreement with committed capacity and a quality protocol |
| **Brand-building ability** | Demonstrated | The brand system itself is credible evidence |
| **Prior operating experience** | Reduces execution risk materially | Any prior scaled consumer or D2C operating role |

**If the founding team lacks FMCG distribution experience, hire it before
raising the seed.** It is the single highest-leverage addition to this deck —
worth more than any improvement to the projections.

## The three questions that decide everything

Ranked by how much they matter, and by how cheaply they can be answered:

1. **Does it taste better than Misfits, blind?** ~₹4 L, 6 weeks. Answer this
   before spending anything else.
2. **Do 35% of first buyers come back within 90 days?** ~₹25 L of inventory
   and media, 6 months.
3. **Can it hold 4 units/store/week in 50 stores?** ~₹8 L, 8 weeks.

Approximately **₹40 L and nine months** converts this from a 5.7/10 to a
7.5–8/10. That is the actual fundraise to run now.
