## 2. Content pillars

The ten pillars from the brief, grouped into the six buckets that set the mix:

| Bucket | Share | Pillars inside it | Formats that work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sweet-making process** | 40% | Sweet making process · Behind the scenes · Factory tours · Product close-ups | Slow-motion Reels, macro photos, process carousels |
| **Packaging** | 20% | Packaging videos · Festival campaigns (retail) | ASMR Reels, "anatomy of a box" carousels |
| **Wedding gifting** | 15% | Wedding gifting | Monogram reveals, hamper builds, planner carousels |
| **Corporate gifting** | 10% | Corporate gifting · Festival campaigns (corporate) | Logo-foil Reels, HR checklists, dispatch time-lapses |
| **Founder journey** | 10% | Founder story | Talking-head Reels, story carousels, takeovers |
| **Testimonials** | 5% | Customer reactions | Reaction Reels, reshared unboxings |

### Reel style guide

- **Cinematic:** tripod, one idea per shot, slow motion at 120 fps for pours, cuts, ribbons and varq.
- **Signature shots:** pouring milk · the ladle's first stir · grain forming · a warm knife cutting milk cake to reveal the caramel · silver varq laid on barfi · a lid closing and a ribbon pulled tight · a gift box opened on camera · a diya lit beside a thali · a bride's family at a tasting.
- **Hook in the first second**, on screen as text *and* in the first visual. No logo intros.
- **Length:** 7–15 s for process/ASMR, 20–35 s for stories and founder pieces.
- **Sound:** real ASMR sound for process; trending instrumental for montages; burned-in captions on anything with speech.
- **End card:** 1 s, logo + line. Every Reel also goes to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.

## 4. Growth tactics

1. **Local food creators (Phase 2, from week 5).** 5–8 Prayagraj/Lucknow/Varanasi creators (5k–100k followers) per month. Offer a kitchen visit + a gift box; ask for a Reel shot in the kitchen, published as an **Instagram Collab post** (appears on both profiles). Disclose paid partnerships with the paid-partnership label — ASCI's influencer guidelines require it.
2. **Wedding planners & venues.** Tag every planner and venue on wedding posts (with permission) and co-post hamper builds. They reshare because it makes them look good.
3. **Corporate LinkedIn crossover.** Every corporate Reel is also posted on the founder's LinkedIn — that is where HR and procurement actually are.
4. **Comment-to-DM keywords.** Captions invite *DIWALI, WEDDING, LOGO, CHECKLIST, ROYAL*. Set up auto-replies (Meta's built-in automation or ManyChat) to send the matching catalogue + enquiry link. Every one of these is a lead.
5. **Transparent giveaways, sparingly.** One per festival at most (e.g. post #20), with stated rules, a real winner, and no "follow 10 accounts" chains.
6. **UGC loop.** A small card in every box: *"Opened something beautiful? Tag @mithaiwallahofficial."* Reshare the best (with permission) to Stories and the Reviews highlight.
7. **Paid amplification (Phase 3+).** Boost only posts that already out-perform organically. Target: 25–55, Prayagraj + 50 km for retail; job titles/interests (HR, admin, procurement) in Lucknow, Noida, Delhi NCR for corporate; engaged/newly-engaged in UP for weddings. Send ads to **WhatsApp** (click-to-WhatsApp) — the highest-converting destination in India.

## 5. DMs and comments → leads

| Trigger | Auto-reply (then a human within 2 business hours) |
|---|---|
| "DIWALI" / "GIFTING" | "Thank you! Here's our corporate catalogue: mithaiwallah.in/brochure/corporate — share your headcount and dates here or on WhatsApp and our team will call you today." |
| "WEDDING" | "Congratulations! 🎉 Our wedding catalogue: mithaiwallah.in/brochure/wedding — when is the big day?" |
| "LOGO" | "Send us your logo file (PDF/SVG) and we'll show you a mock-up on a Mithaiwallah box." |
| Price question in comments | Reply publicly: "Prices depend on quantity and customisation — we've sent you a DM 🙏" and DM the enquiry link. Never argue price in comments. |
| Complaint | Reply publicly within 1 hour: acknowledge, move to DM/phone, resolve, then (if they're happy) ask them to update the comment. |

Every DM conversation that becomes a real enquiry is logged in the lead form (the team can submit it on the customer's behalf with `utm_source=instagram`), so Instagram's contribution shows up in the lead dashboard.
