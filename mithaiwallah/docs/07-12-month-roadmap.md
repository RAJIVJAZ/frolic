# 07 · 12-Month Execution Roadmap

**Positioning for all twelve months:** Mithaiwallah is a *Premium Celebration & Gifting Brand* — serving weddings, festivals, corporates and families with customised luxury sweet experiences — not just a sweet manufacturer.

> **Calendar alignment.** If Month 1 is **October 2026**, Diwali (8 Nov 2026) and the wedding season (late Nov 2026 – Feb 2027) both fall inside Phases 1–2 — *before* the phases dedicated to them. Don't wait: run a **lite corporate push** (Month 1: warm network, SMEs in Prayagraj, the announcement bar) and a **lite wedding push** (Months 2–3: planners and venues in Prayagraj) alongside the foundation work. Phases 3 and 4 then build the full programmes for **Diwali 2027** and the **2027–28 wedding season**, when buyers actually decide (corporate: July–September; wedding planners: 3–6 months ahead).

## Phase 5 · Months 10–12 · Scale

**Objective:** grow beyond Prayagraj without diluting the product.

| Track | Action | Gate before starting |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor network | Distributors / super-stockists across **UP & MP** for the shelf-stable range | Shelf life validated by lab testing; retail packs with batch codes; written margin & credit terms |
| Franchise | Boutique format pilot (1–2 outlets) with early partners | Unit economics proven on a company-run outlet or kiosk; franchise agreement reviewed by counsel |
| Marketplaces | Amazon, Flipkart, and quick-commerce (for local city delivery) for shelf-stable SKUs | Packaging passes transit tests; FSSAI labelling compliant; margin after commissions positive |
| Export | Respond to diaspora importer enquiries (UAE, UK, US, Canada) | Product-specific shelf-life data; export registration and destination-market rules checked |
| National campaigns | Diwali 2027 national campaign; creator collaborations in metros; paid social to NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru | Fulfilment capacity for 3× the previous peak |

**KPIs by end of Month 12:** 10+ distributors · franchise pilot live (or a clear no-go decision) · marketplace listings live · national Diwali campaign executed · Mithaiwallah recognised as a leading premium sweets and gifting brand in Uttar Pradesh.

## Risks to watch

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Festive demand exceeds kitchen capacity | Cap corporate orders per week in the booking system; early-bird windows; staff and packaging ordered in August |
| Quality slips at volume | Batch tasting sign-off, batch codes, customer feedback loop in WhatsApp |
| Shelf life too short for distance | Route fresh sweets (kalakand, malai barfi) locally only; ship the shelf-stable range nationally |
| Brand name conflict | Trademark search and logo registration before signage (doc 01 §4) |
| Fake-review temptation | Don't. Real reviews compound; fake ones get the Business Profile suspended |
